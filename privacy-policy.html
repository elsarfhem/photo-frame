Easy Photo Frame - Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 15, 2026

Overview

Easy Photo Frame is a photo slideshow application for Android. This policy describes how the app handles your data.

Data Collection

Easy Photo Frame does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to external servers. All data remains on your device.

Amazon Photos Integration

When you connect your Amazon Photos account:

  • The app uses Login with Amazon to authenticate your account.
  • OAuth tokens are stored securely on your device using Android Keystore encryption.
  • The app accesses your Amazon Photos albums and images solely to display them in the slideshow.
  • No photos or account data are uploaded, shared, or transmitted to any third party.
  • You can disconnect your Amazon account at any time from the app settings, which deletes all stored tokens.

Network Access

The app accesses your local network (SMB shares) and Amazon Photos API exclusively to retrieve photos for display. No data is sent to any analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Permissions

  • Internet: Required to connect to Amazon Photos and SMB network shares.
  • Network State: Used to detect connectivity changes.
  • Wake Lock: Keeps the screen on during slideshow playback.

Data Storage

All app data (settings, cached images, authentication tokens) is stored locally on your device. No cloud backup of app data is performed by the app itself.

Children's Privacy

This app does not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

Updates to this policy will be reflected on this page with a revised date.

Contact
