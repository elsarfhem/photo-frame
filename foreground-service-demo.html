Photo Frame - Foreground Service Usage

Permission: FOREGROUND_SERVICE_SPECIAL_USE

Purpose

Photo Frame is a 24/7 digital photo frame kiosk application designed for continuous unattended operation on Android tablets. The foreground service (SlideshowWatchdog) serves two critical purposes:

  1. Process protection: Prevents Android from terminating the app during extended operation, ensuring the photo slideshow runs uninterrupted for days or weeks.
  2. Health monitoring: Detects when the slideshow stalls (e.g., due to network issues loading photos from SMB shares) and automatically recovers by advancing to the next photo or restarting the slideshow.

Demo Video

This video shows the app running a photo slideshow with the foreground service active. The notification shade is opened to show the "Photo Frame Active — Monitoring slideshow" persistent notification.

When the service runs

  • Started when the user presses Play to begin the slideshow
  • Stopped when the user presses Pause or exits the app
  • Only active while the slideshow is playing — never runs in the background without user intent

Why SPECIAL_USE

This use case does not fit standard foreground service types. The service does not sync data, play media, track location, or connect to external devices. It monitors an on-screen slideshow for stalls and prevents process termination during kiosk-mode operation.
